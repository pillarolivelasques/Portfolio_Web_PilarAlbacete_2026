Every project starts differently

Some begin with a wedding. Others with a new business, a meaningful place, a favorite restaurant, or a family tradition. No two projects ever look the same — and I hope they never do. Whether it's a bespoke commission or something beautifully simple, each piece is thoughtfully created to feel personal, timeless, and made to last.

Places have always inspired me. From Christ the Redeemer to the gardens of Toledo and the beaches of Bahia, I love translating places into artwork that people can keep long after they've left.

At the heart of it all, I'm simply creating pieces people will want to keep.

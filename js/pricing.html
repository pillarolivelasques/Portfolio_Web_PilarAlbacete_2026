Live Painting

Watch it become art, in real time

How it works

Your celebration, painted as it happens

I set up an easel right at your event — ceremony, reception, or party — and paint live while everything unfolds around me. Guests get to watch the piece come together in real time, and you're left with a one-of-a-kind memory of that exact day, painted from life, not from a photo afterward.

I can paint the ceremony itself as it happens, or set up during the reception to paint your guests live — a fun, interactive moment everyone remembers, and a keepsake for you both.

It works beautifully for weddings, but also birthdays, anniversaries, launches — any moment worth capturing as it's actually happening.
